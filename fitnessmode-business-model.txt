Business Model Canvas - Personal Trainer MVP
1. Value Propositions
For Personal Trainers:
* Free Digital Footprint: Get a professional landing page optimized for local Google search without paying web hosting costs.
* Zero Commission: Keep 100% of client payments with no lead fees.
* Social Media Traffic: Drive motivated clients directly to their Instagram or TikTok feeds where their best content already lives.
For Clients:
* Frictionless Discovery: Search local trainers by city, modality (home, gym, outdoor, online), and rates without creating an account.
* Social Proof First: Preview a trainer's active social feed before reaching out to verify their style and real client results.
2. Customer Segments
* Supply Side (Trainers): Freelance personal trainers, athletic coaches, and online fitness trainers looking for extra client visibility.
* Demand Side (Clients): People wanting structured workout routines, weight loss guidance, injury recovery, or accountability in their local area.
3. Channels
* Trainer Acquisition: Direct DM outreach on Instagram, partnerships with fitness academies/universities, and referral links shared by early adopters.
* Client Acquisition: Local Google SEO targeting terms like "Personal trainer in [City Name]" and social shares from listed trainers.
4. Customer Relationships
* Self-Service & Automated: Fast, 3-step signup requiring zero manual assistance.
* Value Demonstration: Automated monthly emails sent to trainers reporting profile views and outbound social media clicks.
5. Revenue Streams
* MVP Phase ($0): Free listing model to rapidly cross the 100+ trainer milestone and trigger network effects.
* Phase 2 Gate (Future Upsell): Pro Tier Subscription ($10 - $20/month) unlocking direct WhatsApp & Call buttons, featured top-of-search placement, and a "Verified Trainer" trust badge.
6. Key Resources
* Tech Stack: High-speed directory built on React.js, Supabase, typescript and tailwindcss 4.
* Data Asset: A clean, searchable database of active, responsive local trainers.
7. Key Activities
* Supply Growth: Aggressive outreach to hit 100+ trainers in target cities.
* SEO Engineering: Generating dynamic programmatically-created city pages (e.g., /trainers/madrid, /trainers/miami).
* Moderation: Filtering out inactive or spam profiles to maintain directory quality.
8. Key Partners
* Fitness Academies: Certification schools looking to offer job placement or marketing tools to newly certified trainers.
* Geocoding APIs: Mapbox or Google Maps for location autocomplete and distance calculations.
9. Cost Structure
* Infrastructure: Minimal cloud costs (Vercel, Supabase PostgreSQL).
* Outreach: Founder time and direct social messaging.
